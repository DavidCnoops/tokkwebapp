Users are members of your Tokk group. You can share to-do lists with them, and generate reports for their tracked time if your account has the permission to do so. You can invite new users via Settings › Invitations.

Active users are users that can log in and use their account.
Each active user costs $5 USD per month.

Archived users are users whose account is disabled. They cannot log in, but their tracked time can still be included in reports. Archived users are free of charge.

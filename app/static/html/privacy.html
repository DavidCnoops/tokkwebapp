Privacy Policy

  • "We", "us" or "our" refers to wolkvolk bvba, the company that owns Tokk.
  • "You" or "your" refers to the user or company that has signed up for or makes use of Tokk.
  • "Service" or "services" refers to the websites, applications and content that make up Tokk.
  • "Website" or "websites" refers to tokkapp.com and tokk.be.
2. Data collection and cookie policy

We take privacy very seriously.

You will always be in charge of your own data.
Your data will not be shared with third parties.
We keep the data collection to an absolute minimum.
The data we collect will only be used for the proper functioning of the service.
Cookies are used for login purposes only.

We strictly rely on people using the premium version of the service for our income. Therefore no advertising will be done, nor will your data be collected for advertising purposes.

3. Security

We use HTTPS and SSL for secure connections. We take measures to make sure that all data you provide using our services is as safe as possible.

4. Passwords

Passwords are stored in our database in an encrypted way. In case of an unlikely security breach, nobody will be able to read your password, not even us.

5. Payment details

We use Stripe to handle our payments. They have bank-level security (AES-256 encryption) to protect sensitive data like your payment details. They are PCI level 1 compliant. This is the strictest level of certification available in the payments industry.
